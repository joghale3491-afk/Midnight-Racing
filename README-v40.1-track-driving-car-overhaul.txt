MIDNIGHT RACING v40.1 — TRACK BOUNDARIES, STREET SCENERY, HANDLING & CAR DESIGN

Built from the working v40.0 package.

CHANGES
- Added a hard off-track boundary to Player 1 and local Player 2. Cars can use the road shoulder but cannot drive out into the surrounding terrain.
- Added continuous, closer-spaced safety guardrails to all track themes, including coastal, forest, desert and other natural courses.
- Smoothed steering response and reduced the abrupt turn rate. Keyboard mapping is explicitly A/Left = steer left and D/Right = steer right.
- Expanded urban street frontage with instanced buildings, illuminated windows, neon shop signs and awnings while keeping background skyline elements further away.
- Cleared the power-up event feed and active power-up toast when a race ends or the player returns to the menu. Power-up events are ignored outside active races.
- Refined the body outline and reduced the dark wheel centre disc so tyre rubber, spokes and brake hardware are more distinct.
- Increased body/chassis and wheelbase variation across the seven rarity classes. Higher classes gain lower/wider proportions and progressively more motorsport-inspired aero.
- Renamed generic body families to recognizable vehicle categories (hot hatch, muscle coupe, GT, roadster, rally, prototype, supercar and hypercar). These are original, brand-free designs inspired by broad real-world vehicle types, not exact replicas or copied meshes.
- Bumped service-worker cache to v40.1.

PRESERVED
- Y8 SDK/App ID integration, Supabase project configuration, existing profile saves, multiplayer, race modes, tracks, quests, power-ups, and countdown fix.

CHECKS
- JavaScript syntax, CSS parsing, HTML structure, archive integrity, duplicate HTML IDs, local-file references and presence of Y8/Supabase setup are checked during packaging.
- A live browser/graphics test is still required for driving feel and visual appearance.
